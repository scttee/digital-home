# Chapter One: Reclaiming Space

The web used to feel like building. 

Now it feels like renting.

We post our thoughts to platforms we don't own. We share our photos on services that can disappear. We build our connections through apps that monetize our attention.

What if we owned our corners again?

---

# Chapter Two: The Alternative

This is an experiment in digital sovereignty.

A personal website that replaces social media. A home on the web that I actually control.

No algorithms deciding what you see. No engagement metrics. No ads. Just presence, intentionality, and connection on my terms.

---

# Chapter Three: What It Means

Building a digital home means:

- Owning your data completely
- Designing your own interface
- Choosing who connects with you
- Creating at your own pace
- Existing outside the attention economy

It's slower. It's more deliberate. It's worth it.

---

# Epilogue

If you're reading this, you've found my corner of the web.

Welcome home.
